# Steam Store Copy

## Game Title

Xingming Shuihu: Snowbound Vengeance

## Short Description

Rally forty outlaws, master a three-slot party and four combat disciplines, and carve your own path through a Water Margin-inspired turn-based RPG.

## Long Description

### About This Game

Xingming Shuihu: Snowbound Vengeance is a single-player turn-based RPG inspired by the public-domain characters and turbulent world of Water Margin. Begin with Lin Chong's wrongful exile and travel from the frozen streets of Bianjing to the fortified waters of Liangshan.

Combat combines a three-slot formation with four opposing disciplines. Guard pressure, stagger, bleeding, armor break, turn order, and Yìqì all shape each encounter. Chain different disciplines to fill the constellation gauge and unleash a party-wide Starbound Combo.

Take direct control or enable the tactical auto-battler with `1x / 2x / 4x` speed. Balanced, Breaker, Guardian, and Hunter doctrines apply different priorities for healing, guard skills, resource spending, and target selection while preserving meaningful party-building decisions.

### Features

- A nine-chapter campaign with Brotherhood, Allegiance, and Renunciation endings.
- Forty recruitable heroes with unique skills, ultimates, permanent mastery trials, and two switchable destiny doctrines.
- One hundred and twenty personal side stories and forty capstone legend battles, each with a dedicated encounter rule, narrative payoff, and unique equipment reward.
- Twenty-eight recruitment scrolls. The four-part Yang Zhi, Gongsun Sheng, Lu Junyi, and Zhang Shun chain joins standalone oaths for Shi Jin, Qin Ming, Chai Jin, Guan Sheng, Zhang Qing, and Xu Ning, plus trials for Sun Erniang, Gu Dasao, Ling Zhen, Ruan Xiaoqi, Li Jun, Huyan Zhuo, Liu Tang, Lei Heng, Zhu Tong, Li Ying, Xie Zhen, and Xie Bao. Yang Xiong, Shi Xiu, Sun Li, Yue He, Xiao Rang, and Jiang Jing then close the mid-campaign arc through prison verdicts, rooftop pursuits, snowbound gates, river-signal music, forged orders, and quartermaster defense. Each combines a story choice, a dedicated battle oath, and a permanent roster payoff.
- Three-slot formations, four-way counters, guard breaks, status effects, and ultimate resources.
- Thirty-eight hero bonds with bodyguard assists, momentum chains, stolen-qi healing, flying-stone ricochets, hook-spear armor breaks, naval rescue, Dengzhou hunter chains, half-health rescue, control extension, support-driven guard, opening-strategy bursts, and fourteen dedicated three-hero formations, all recorded in a permanent formation archive.
- Four persistent tactical auto-battle doctrines with three speed settings.
- An endless arena with run edicts, risk-reward encounters every third wave, rotating bosses, and nine permanent relics, plus a weekly fixed-pool challenge. Eight imperial army encounters preview distinct rules such as shield walls, massed volleys, cavalry rushes, linked camps, fire ambushes, night raids, last stands, and sealed meridians, changing each wave's target priority and merit bonus.
- Six optional Starbound Tribulations can be stacked up to three at a time, altering enemy health, armor, attack, speed, hero qi gain, and between-wave recovery in exchange for clearly stated merit, copper, and experience bonuses. Higher best waves unlock increasingly hostile rules.
- Twelve permanent formation discoveries and three weekly merit seals.
- Nineteen cross-playthrough achievements covering story, hero doctrines, formation and army-encounter discoveries, camp growth, chapter medals, and challenge play, with live counters and progress bars on every locked goal.
- A Starbound Wayfinder that reads the current save and points to the most useful next goal across the campaign, recruitment, personal stories, arena, camp, relics, formations, doctrines, and endings. The ending screen also summarizes six long-term collection tracks.
- Twenty-seven chapter medals that reward no-KO runs, Starbound Combos, and fast clears on replay.
- Autosave, import/export, save migration, and New Game+ archive inheritance.
- Adjustable music and effects volume plus reduced-motion support.

## Mature Content

The game contains cold-weapon combat, blood, imprisonment, wrongful prosecution, and themes of political oppression. It does not contain photorealistic dismemberment or sexual content.

## Generative AI Disclosure

During development, the team used a ComfyUI pipeline to pre-generate selected character, environment, enemy, UI icon-sheet, title-video, music, and combat sound-effect assets. Human developers reviewed, corrected, composed, and mastered the final results. The shipped game does not generate content at runtime, does not require a ComfyUI connection, and does not send saves or personal data to a generative model.
